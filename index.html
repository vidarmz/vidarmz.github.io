ABOUT ME

I'm Vida Ramezanian, an AI Engineer with 3+ years of experience building production LLM, search, and NLP systems. At Winsoo I work on Persian content moderation, semantic search, and a billboard-creation chatbot. Before that I built RAG/GraphRAG assistants at Bitazza and an AI talent-matching platform and banking assistants at Farazpardazan. I hold an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence from Sharif University of Technology, where my research covered computer vision (uncertainty-aware monocular depth estimation, video instance segmentation, knowledge distillation) and Persian NLP, and I have published at EACL and TMLR.

Projects

Persian Content Moderation (Winsoo) - A text and image moderation system that flags prohibited ads using rules, embeddings, and LLM/vision-model checks, sending uncertain cases to human reviewers.

Shared Model Hub (Winsoo) - A shared service (LiteLLM, Ollama, Triton) that serves embedding, LLM, and vision models to several products.

Semantic Search and Billboard Chatbot (Winsoo) - Semantic search for Persian/English ads, and a LangGraph chatbot that helps users create billboard ads.

RAG and GraphRAG Assistant (Bitazza) - A production conversational assistant using LLaMA, LangGraph, retrieval, and a knowledge graph, plus product tagging and vector search over large catalogs.

Talz.io and ENMB Banking Assistant (Farazpardazan) - An AI talent-matching platform built on embeddings and semantic search, and LLM-powered conversational assistants for a banking app.

Uncertainty-aware Monocular Depth Estimation (MSc thesis) - Estimating the uncertainty of depth models and using it in training to make existing monocular depth estimators more reliable.

Video Instance Segmentation and Knowledge Distillation - Improved weakly supervised video instance segmentation with better clustering and feature extraction, and co-authored a survey on knowledge distillation.

Persian Colloquial-to-Formal Conversion and Question Answering - Built a dataset and models for converting colloquial Persian to formal text, and benchmarked Persian question-answering systems.

Hand Motion Classification from EMG Signals (BSc thesis) - Designed an 8-channel wireless EMG acquisition board, collected a dataset of five hand motions, and trained machine learning models to recognize them.
